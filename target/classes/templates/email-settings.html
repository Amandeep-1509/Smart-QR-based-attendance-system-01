📖 How to get a Google App Password

1
Log in to your Google Account and visit Security settings.
2
Make sure 2-Step Verification is switched ON.
3
Search for App Passwords (or visit google.com/apppasswords ↗ ).
4
Create an app name (e.g., Attendance System) and click Create.
5
Copy the generated 16-character password and paste it into the form on the left.
💡 Note: Never use your regular Gmail sign-in password. Google requires App Passwords for third-party SMTP.
